## Quick Pitch

When a buried alien organism known as the Infinitysombiotes is awakened beneath the Earth, a rogue military program weaponizes it to create an army of godlike hosts. Each symbiote does not simply grant power—it multiplies the host's strength by infinity to the power of 5555, turning humans into titan-level beings with catastrophic destructive force.

A haunted former soldier and a brilliant archaeologist must stop the final fusion before the symbiote intelligence turns humanity into a new cosmic order of divine destruction.
